# Repository contribution rules

- Preserve the distinction between documentary reports, raw evidence, calculations, qualification results, failed experiments, and independent replication. Never invent scientific evidence, files, logs, plots, hashes, or results.
- Do not upgrade an experiment's status or convert `DOCUMENTARY_ONLY`, missing source data, failed qualification, or an unrun experiment into a verified result. Keep historical claims as written; put corrections in clearly labeled audit or status material.
- Do not force-push `main`, rewrite its history, merge a pull request without explicit authorization, or close, delete, or rewrite research branches and PRs without explicit authorization.
- Do not delete or replace research records or source documents without explicit authorization. For archive integrations, retain provenance and link to a single canonical copy when exact duplicates are intentionally excluded.
- Do not upload the reported 2.14 GB original data package or other raw research data to this public repository without explicit user confirmation and a completed privacy, credential, third-party-rights, and holdout-data review.
- Keep missing-source states such as `NOT_IMPORTED` and `POINTER_ONLY_UNTRANSFERABLE` explicit. A filename or source pointer is not evidence that its bytes were transferred.
- Report tests only when they were actually run; distinguish repository unit tests from scientific reproduction.
