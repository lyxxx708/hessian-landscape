## Cause: CG failure versus operator precision

These are separate problems.

- **Preconditioner:** the frozen Hutchinson diagonal makes V0 fail on DLN at γ=0.001 (full solve hits 1000 iterations) and raises cost by up to 36x on MLP (431 vs 12 iterations). Removing it gives 30 of 36 valid even in FP32; the other 6 fail the tight solve, which is the precision limit below.
- **FP32 precision:** plain FP32 stagnates near 3e-6 relative residual, above the 1e-6 tight and probe tolerance. FP64 clears this on every DLN case. Measured FP32 vs FP64 relative error: GGN matvec 5e-8 to 7e-7, gradient 9e-8 to 4e-7.

The mechanism (diagonal mixing range and nullspace of the DLN GGN) is a hypothesis and was not separately proven.

## New finding: FP32 can pass validity with a wrong δ

On `dln_d8_w16_orthogonal_balanced_c1`, seed 3, checkpoint 3 (|g| ≈ 2.9e-7), FP32 reports NUMERIC_VALID with `delta_stability` passing (1e-8 to 3e-6), but δ is wrong:

| γ | FP32 δ | Dense δ | Abs error | V3 agrees with dense |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | 0.1772 | 0.1461 | 3.1e-2 (21%) | yes |
| 0.01 | 0.2392 | 0.2307 | 8.5e-3 | yes |
| 0.001 (V1 only) | 0.1515 | 0.1532 | 1.7e-3 | yes |

At this gradient size the FP32 gradient itself is off by about 10%, so the error enters before CG starts and the convergence and stability checks cannot see it. This is the only such state among the 36 tested. The next smallest gradient (1.4e-4) has error 4.7e-6, and there is no data between those two gradient sizes.

**v2 exposure:** among the 273 COMPLETE coarse v2 measurements so far, 6 have |g| < 1e-5 and 2 more have |g| < 1e-4. Those results are not removed or edited, but they should be flagged and audited offline before any v2 claim relies on them.

## Estimands and matched-cost baseline

Excluding that one state, FP32 and FP64 agree closely. Median relative differences between V1 and V3 (101 paired DLN rows): δ 1.9e-6, E_task 6e-7, E_val 7e-7, cheap_rho 3e-7, F* 2e-7. All maxima (δ 0.21, others 0.07 to 0.10) come from the same state.

**Matched-cost CG does not need redefining.** Its formula is unchanged, but its budget shrinks because it is tied to solve cost: 52% median on DLN V0→V3, 1964→220 on MLP γ=0.001. Predictor features shift, so S1 measurements and the predictor must be regenerated under v2.1, as already planned.

**Scientific risk, not fixed by this repair:** matched CG saturates. It converged in 9 to 14 matvecs on budgets of 154 to 1964 on MLP and ResNet, with gain equal to F* to the printed precision. On DLN the V0→V3 matched-gain change is a median of 2.6e-7. So the baseline has low discrimination, which may limit S1/S2 signal under either protocol. I am reporting this, not tuning it.

## Remaining risks

1. **Transformer unqualified.** This was the blocker at first writing; the V3 CPU numeric part has since passed (`frozen_transformer_long.jsonl`, see "Final repair judgment"). CUDA G0 remains open. V0 and V1 were not rerun, because they do not affect the decision.
2. **No trained non-DLN S1 checkpoints exist.** MLP, ResNet and Transformer evidence is from one-step preflight states, one per family. MLP starts at queue ordinal 1876, ResNet 2626, Transformer 2876; the queue is at about 878. One reduced-budget dev MLP state agreed (V0 and V1 at γ=0.001 both valid, δ differ by 6e-7), but no trained ResNet or Transformer state has been tested. Low-gradient, trained states are where FP32 went wrong and are untested outside DLN.
3. **FP64 VRAM unmeasured.** FP32 G0 peak: Transformer 1.91 GB, target 9.5 GiB. Doubling suggests about 3.8 GB, but that is an estimate. Only real CUDA G0 can settle it.
4. **FP64 GPU throughput unmeasured.** It may change the feasible study length.
5. **γ=0.001 structure measures in v2.1 need separate verification.** Solves now converge on DLN, but the structure verdicts themselves have not been recomputed.
6. **Provenance-mismatch run stays isolated.** Run `s1-dln_d4_w16_orthogonal_balanced_c1-s00-44e78b87532803bf` stays out, with 24 blocked dependents. Its setting has 4 of 5 trajectories in v2, and v2.1 would have to retrain it, not copy it.
7. **Harness only.** The v2.1 worktree has experimental plumbing and is uncommitted. It must be reverted to the config-only diff before freezing.

## Final repair judgment (2026-11-04)

**Verdict stays NO-GO for switching; v2 continues.** V3 now has CPU numeric evidence on all four families at γ=0.001 and 0.01, and fixes the failure the study actually hit. That is evidence for the repair, not production qualification.

Still open for Transformer and v2.1, none of which CPU can answer: GPU cost per GGNv, total GPU solve time, FP64 VRAM, production feasibility, and trained non-DLN states. Transformer, ResNet and MLP evidence remains one preflight state each. Real CUDA G0 requires a cooperative v2 pause, which has not been taken.

**New v2.1 requirement.** Synthetic DLN data bytes depend on the BLAS thread count (`evidence/v2_numeric_audit/ADDENDUM_01_thread_pinning.md`), which also explains the isolated seed-0 run. The v2.1 data lock must make generation independent of threads, or store the data, or pin and record the thread environment. In v2, every relaunch must keep using `genesis_env.ps1` (OMP=2), or DLN resume will fail.

**Correction:** the section above says the FP32 gradient on the false-valid state is off "about 10%". The audit measured a 19% norm difference and 41% vector error (cosine 0.928), and v2's recorded δ at γ=0.1 is 0.1794; the 0.1772 above is a CPU FP32 recomputation.

**v2.1 protocol item:** drop the coarse/fine jobs that repeat a partition on DLN and MLP (1,260 of 3,120 S1 jobs), under its own lock. v2 keeps them.

**P0 FP32 audit:** interim result 302 of 305 distinct measurements AGREE, 3 MATERIAL, all from one trajectory (`AUDIT_REPORT_2026-11-04.md`). Protocol and results are in `evidence/v2_numeric_audit/`. v2 labels and receipts stay as they are; audit findings are disclosed separately in the final report, and unreviewed data is not to be described as high-precision validated.
