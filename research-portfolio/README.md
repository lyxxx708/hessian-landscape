## Non-negotiable scientific distinctions

- **Measurement / numerical check / qualification / adjudication / independent confirmation** are different states.
- A null-model *qualification failure* cannot be reported as rejection of the scientific null.
- Multiple windows, probes and model depths sharing a seed do not increase the independent replicate count.
- Historical PASS/FAIL assertions are tied to their specific protocol version; later audits may supersede them.
- Merely locating a file in another application does not mean its bytes have been archived here.
