## Scientific relationship without false equivalence

Hessian geometry affects linearized optimization dynamics under suitable conditions, but block structure of (H+λI)⁻¹, local dynamical relaxation, and adaptive empirical IAT are three different observables. A nondecaying inverse-Hessian distance profile does not by itself imply anti-slowing, and a global pseudo-IAT jump does not imply a Hessian phase transition. No retrieved source establishes a causal cross-project bridge.
