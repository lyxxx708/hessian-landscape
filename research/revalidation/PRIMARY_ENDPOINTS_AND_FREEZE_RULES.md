# Primary endpoints, evidence gates and freeze policy — LOCAL FULL v1

**Pre-registration draft:** 2026-11-02; MUST be frozen and hashed before any S1 scientific measurements. Do not revise based on outcomes. The 2026-10-26 G0–G4 contract is useful background; this file states the concrete local-run decisions. If a defect makes an endpoint impossible, version the protocol, record why, and rerun affected discovery/confirmation rather than post-hoc changing a threshold.

## Population, independence, and measurement objects

- Each model setting is an architecture, dataset, depth, parameter grouping, training budget, loss scaling and fixed hyperparameter recipe. **Training seed cluster** means a separately generated initialization, data-order/RNG, teacher/data draw (for DLN) and optimizer trajectory. Different checkpoints, damping values and probe streams within a cluster are correlated repeated measurements.
- Vision and text tasks may reuse the same public corpus across seeds; these seed-cluster CIs do **not** establish generalization to independent datasets. Keep that limitation prominent. No S1 seed is allowed in S2; S2 cannot be used for tuning.
- A natural checkpoint is generated by training **the same loss and target** from a fixed starting state. An artificially changed zero-residual target is G0-only, not a training checkpoint. For dropout and stochastic training layers, freeze randomness and module state within one curvature operator evaluation.
- Primary SPD object: correct loss-specific GGN G, Q=G+lambda I, D=blockdiag(Q); one same-batch gradient g. Exact-H indefinite comparisons are labeled M exploratory and never substitute for SPD utility.

## Primary U endpoint and diagnostics

At each numerically eligible state, p_star = -Q^-1 g, p_block = -D^-1 g, alpha_oracle = -(g^T p_block)/(p_block^T Q p_block). Let F_star=-(g^T p_star)/2 and F_block=(g^T p_block)^2/[2(p_block^T Q p_block)]. Define eta=F_block/F_star, delta=1-eta in [0,1]. E_task and held-out E_val are distinct response metrics. Do not compute alpha from validation data, and do not confuse this offline quadratic oracle with trainable online step control.

**Utility scientific signal in S2:** at least two non-identical/nontrivial predeclared conditions, including at least one nonlinear model, must have a seed-cluster median delta >=0.05 and 95% cluster-bootstrap lower confidence bound >0.01 under at least two of three predeclared relative damping ratios. Report other lambda results in full. The family-wise confirmatory p-values across the five S2 anchor conditions use Holm correction; call this a *project Go threshold*, not a universal statistical law. If optimizer target is vision + text, demand observed positive evidence in both domains before extending S3 to a universal vision/text claim.

**Predictor signal:** train only on S1. Define class label positive when delta>0.05. In S2 with enough positive and negative independent seed clusters, require AUROC >=0.75 and improvement >=0.10 over the best predeclared cheap baseline (lambda/scale, gradient norm, approximate conditioning, block energy, same-cost short CG). Also report PR-AUC, calibration and seed-group uncertainty. If S2 class prevalence makes AUROC undefined, mark the classification gate NOT_EVALUABLE and fall back only to a PRE-FROZEN continuous endpoint: Spearman >=0.5 and MAE reduction >=10% over the best baseline on S2, with seed-level bootstrap direction consistent. Do not tune thresholds on S2.

**Optimizer gate:** S3 new-method comparison is conditional on utility + predictor gates and at least one credible real nonlinear line-search contrast; a synthetic three-seed value or a Frobenius heatmap alone is not sufficient. Equal compute/tuning budgets and end-to-end wall-clock time are mandatory. If utility fails, report S3 STOP, not a missing experiment.

## Primary S structural endpoint

For a fitted checkpoint with L>=8 correctly ordered layer groups, obtain block-pair squared Frobenius energies E_ij for R=(G+lambda I)^-1. For each k, use normalized **per-element** RMS c(k) = sqrt( E_k / sum_{|i-j|=k}(n_i*n_j) ) divided by the same expression at k=0. Report both raw and identity-corrected resolvent energy when defined; do not confuse large diagonal lambda^-1 I with true layer coupling.

Define a finite-depth far-tail panel K_far = {ceil(L/2),...,L-2}. Exclude k=L-1 from the primary tail shape because only one unordered pair contributes, but still **report** that bin. Define weighted tail-CV from c(k), with weights proportional to the number of ordered layer pairs. A **candidate finite-depth flat tail** meets CV <=0.15 AND mean(c(K_far)) >=0.02 AND the corresponding far-layer energies exceed numerical CG-bias and probe-confidence noise floors. These numbers are discovery screening thresholds, NOT proofs of an infinite-depth law. Report sensitivity of results to c threshold 0.01/0.05 and CV 0.10/0.20.

The primary substantive questions for S2 are: (a) does such a nontrivial finite-depth tail persist on new independent seeds under at least two lambda values in nonorthogonal/real nonlinear families; (b) how do c_tail and off-diagonal mass scale with L at matched function scale and damping; (c) how much survives gauge/normalization/architecture nulls? A generic approximately uniform global projector can pass (a) while the architecture-specific layer-order test FAILS. Treat that as a *generic global mechanism*, not a new architectural distance law.

Fit candidate constant-height, 1/L, 1/sqrt(L), exponential and power-law depth dependence on S1 settings; evaluate predictions on S2 depths. **Do not claim a nonzero L→infinity limit from a finite number of depths.** The confidence statement must be restricted to tested depths/widths/tasks and explicitly address reparameterization dependence.

## M mechanistic evidence

At the same checkpoint compare G, its nullspace or low-rank approximation, R=(G+lambda I)^-1, exact H (tiny models only), residual curvature S=H-G and H+lambda I pole distances. Measure *explained retained residual* rather than simply deleting eigenspaces and showing masked norms. Use rank-matched/spectrum-matched random subspace controls and recorded basis dimension. The horizontal GGN resolvent term is finite at finite lambda and cannot be omitted. Do not claim negative exact-H eigenvalues originate entirely in gauge tangents without a proof/test of the whole eigenspace. Treat λ sweeps as fixed-matrix controls, not dynamical phase transitions.

## Numerical eligibility (checked BEFORE outcome-based selection)

- G0 must verify GGN PSD/symmetric and Q SPD, exact-vs-autograd agreement on small matrices, right loss scaling for MSE and cross entropy, and dense CG/sketch parity. If not, STOP and repair.
- A successful solve needs finite true relative residual <=1e-5 (FP32 large) / 1e-9 (small FP64), plus tolerance-doubling or independent-reference stability: changing solve tolerance by 10x must change delta by <=0.005 absolute and primary distance-bin values by <=10% relative (or within recorded statistical noise). For high condition numbers where this fails, mark NUMERIC_UNRESOLVED; never promote a small algebraic residual as a forward-error guarantee.
- Inverse-block sketch starts at 16 probes/source and doubles to 256, maintaining raw probe energies. Require per-distance 95% uncertainty half-width <=20% of eligible positive signal OR report UNCERTAIN; do not turn noisy small values into zeros or infer a plateau. Different probe seeds are Monte Carlo replication, NOT new training seeds.
- Ineligibility rules: same checkpoint target/batch changed, Jacobian/gradient norm below pre-frozen threshold, solver inconsistency, invalid loss, nonfinite, insufficient estimator precision, incorrect weights, OOM after frozen fallback. Record EVERY invalid case and reason in the denominator, including performance dropouts. Never replace them with better seeds.
- Pre-register number of failed seeds allowed per family for any positive confirmatory claim; default **zero silent losses**, and report scientific PASS only when >=80% of independent training seeds per stated S2 condition are numerically qualified with no missingness bias evidence. If failures correlate with large or small effects, interpret as unqualified irrespective of pass fraction.

## Holdout and optional selection

Always run five fixed S2 anchors at 16 independent seeds each, even when S1 is completely negative. Before S1, create a deterministic, hash-locked seed manifest, dataset split IDs, and analysis code. Additional one top-positive and one top-negative conditions (if eligible) selected by a PRE-REGISTERED ranking rule from S1 may be added with 16 new seeds each; never change that rule after seeing data. Report all exploratory comparisons separately.

Use 2,000 seed-cluster bootstrap replicates for stated intervals; in a 16-seed anchor, do not treat 16×4 checkpoints×3 lambda=192 rows as 192 independent replicates. Report a per-seed aggregate endpoint and the complete repeated-measures picture. Multiple testing corrections and sensitivity analysis must be exposed.
